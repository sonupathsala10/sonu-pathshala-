🧪 Chemistry Formulas

Saare basic formulas ek hi jagah — quick revision ke liye

1️⃣ Mole Concept (मोल संकल्पना)

Moles (n) n = Mass / Molar Mass
Number of Particles N = n × 6.022 × 10²³
Molar Mass M = Mass / Moles

2️⃣ Gas Laws (गैस नियम)

Boyle's Law P₁V₁ = P₂V₂
Charles's Law V₁/T₁ = V₂/T₂
Ideal Gas Equation PV = nRT
Combined Gas Law P₁V₁/T₁ = P₂V₂/T₂

3️⃣ Concentration (सांद्रता)

Molarity (M) M = Moles / Volume (L)
Molality (m) m = Moles / Mass (kg)
Mass % (Mass of solute / Mass of solution) × 100

4️⃣ Acids & Bases (अम्ल एवं क्षारक)

pH pH = -log[H⁺]
pOH pOH = -log[OH⁻]
pH + pOH pH + pOH = 14

5️⃣ Chemical Equations

Balanced Equation Reactants → Products
Avogadro's Number 6.022 × 10²³
Atomic Mass Unit 1 amu = 1.66 × 10⁻²⁴ g

6️⃣ Important Constants (महत्वपूर्ण स्थिरांक)

Avogadro's Number Nₐ = 6.022 × 10²³
Gas Constant R = 8.314 J/mol·K
Speed of Light c = 3 × 10⁸ m/s
Planck's Constant h = 6.626 × 10⁻³⁴ J·s

7️⃣ Common Compounds (सामान्य यौगिक)

Water H₂O
Carbon Dioxide CO₂
Sodium Chloride NaCl
Sulphuric Acid H₂SO₄
Hydrochloric Acid HCl
Sodium Hydroxide NaOH
Calcium Carbonate CaCO₃
Ammonia NH₃
